Photography Direction

Warm tones, soft natural light, elegant movement, and intentional negative space.

Studio Interior
Movement Detail
Class Atmosphere
Lifestyle
Photo Guidelines
Mood

Calm, aspirational, empowering. Natural light preferred. Golden hour warmth. Avoid harsh flash or cold tones.

Composition

Leave space for text overlays. Focus on lines of the body and movement. Minimalist backgrounds, neutral or warm tones.

Tone of Voice

How the LUNA brand speaks to its community.

☾

Elegant

Refined language that reflects the boutique nature of the studio. Poised, never pretentious.

✦

Empowering

Uplifting and encouraging. Celebrates strength, grace, and the journey of every individual.

♥

Warm

Approachable and inviting. Creates a sense of community and belonging.

Voice Examples
Instead of: Sign up for our gym classes today!
Say: Begin your journey toward strength and grace.
Instead of: HURRY! 50% OFF ALL CLASSES!!!
Say: A special moment to begin — enjoy 50% off your first month.

Brand Guidelines

Rules to keep the LUNA brand consistent and recognizable.

Do
  • ✓ Use the full color palette consistently
  • ✓ Maintain minimum clear space around logo
  • ✓ Use warm, golden-tone photography
  • ✓ Keep typography hierarchy consistent
  • ✓ Use celestial motifs (moon, stars) as accents
  • ✓ Write in an elegant, empowering tone
  • ✓ Leave generous whitespace
Don't
  • ✕ Stretch, rotate, or alter the logo
  • ✕ Use neon, saturated, or cold colors
  • ✕ Use casual or aggressive language
  • ✕ Crowd content with too many elements
  • ✕ Mix brand fonts with unrelated typefaces
  • ✕ Use low-resolution or stock-looking imagery
  • ✕ Place the logo on clashing backgrounds
